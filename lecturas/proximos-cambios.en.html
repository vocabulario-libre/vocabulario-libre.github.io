Upcoming changes

What is being made now and what comes after. The work is in the public domain: whoever copies it can carry it on.

Now

  1. The cards in five more languages, for all fifteen lecturas. Done: 1 of 15.
    • Japanese
    • Russian
    • German
    • Sanskrit
    • Avestan

After that

  1. An index of forms: look up a word and see its dictionary form and every place it appears.
  2. Study courses, from the first level to advanced:
    • Spanish
    • English
    • Japanese
  3. Greek:
    • a Greek primer
    • Greek on the cards
  4. Hindi and Chinese on the cards.
  5. A logic pane in the trainer:
    • a course in logic, with problems and solutions
    • the sentences of the texts in formal logic, in each of their readings; which readings agree with modern science and with one another; what follows from them
    • a logic problem each day
  6. More works in the library:
    • Epictetus, «Golden Sayings» (Greek and English)
    • Franklin, «Autobiography» (English)
    • the «Art of War», the «Analects» and the «Dao De Jing» (Chinese)
    • Plato (Greek): the «Apology», «Crito», «Phaedo», «Phaedrus», «Protagoras», «Gorgias», «Theaetetus»
    • the whole Avesta (Avestan)
    • Vedic pieces from the same period as each part of the Avesta, set beside it (Sanskrit and Avestan)
    • a Buddhist work. The first choice is the «Abhidharmakośa». Its Sanskrit text was first printed in the twentieth century, too recently for the project's rules; what is old are its Chinese and Tibetan translations. Which work to include is still to be decided.
