Study

Léxico — vocabulary trainer and reading assistant for the texts of the collection; today, the fifteen readings of the libertadores.

Léxico · Lecturas de los libertadores

  • Every word form the authors wrote, close to thirty thousand, each with the sentence it appears in and an English equivalent; over a thousand Spanish definitions for the hard words. The equivalent follows the word’s sense in that text, not the dictionary’s. Forms, not lemmas: the form is what the reader meets on the page.
  • The text beside its cards: choose paragraphs (or all fifteen texts, through the index) and the cards form. The author’s text and the edition’s apparatus, kept apart.
  • Three card sets: words; atoms — each grammatical word in its place, with its function —; stretches — the smallest run with an equivalent of its own. Atoms and stretches only where the sense is certified; the rest says «under review».
  • Recognise or produce; the language of each side is yours to choose; an index by frequency or spread, in units of a hundred. The interface is in Spanish, English or Japanese.
  • Spaced repetition. Progress is saved in the browser and exported to a file.
  • One file. No network, no install.

The texts

Lecturas de los libertadores

Fifteen texts by three liberators: Bolívar, San Martín, O’Higgins. About forty thousand words from each, 1812–1843: manifestos, speeches, proclamations, dispatches and official letters, public and private letters, maxims. Chronological order. The text is public domain; everything else — editions, apparatus, glosses, programs — is CC0: copy it, host it, adapt it, no condition.

Status, from the governing document (Spanish): Corpus cerrado y publicado (2 de noviembre de 2026).

The standalone pieces were chosen on two criteria of the compiler’s: historical weight and prose. The correspondences and the printed papers, by unit — the whole series to one correspondent, the whole register of one witness, a date window fixed before the pages were read; nothing is omitted inside a unit. Each author has public prose from the campaign years and private letters from his last decades; the word count is reported, not engineered. Only what has a public-domain printed witness, read on the page image, enters. What was considered and not adopted is in the manifest, with its reason. Two of O’Higgins’s texts are not his hand — the 1818 manifesto, drafted by another under his name; the 1823 speech, transmitted by later account; recorded, not corrected. The editions record: they declare their witnesses, note every intervention, pass no judgement.

Each edition: the text, read on the page image against a dated printed witness; the note on the text; the apparatus — restitutions, corrections of reading, divergences, normalisation — with every intervention listed one by one; notes on the sources; a reference key; a glossary; a revision history; numbered paragraphs. “Source” is that text in Markdown, from which the PDF and EPUB are generated. “Register” is the list of checks the edition passed — declaratory, methodological, textual, epistemic, reproducibility — with the proof of each. The editions, the trainer’s interface and the governing document are in Spanish; the glosses, the policy and the README are in English. These are reading editions for private study, not critical editions, and should not be cited as established text.

Documents

Governing document
Identifies and orders the fifteen readings: who signed and who drafted each text, when, from which witnesses it comes and the state of its edition. Spanish, with an English part. EPUB. Source in Markdown.
Scholarship and compliance policy
Standards of Scholarship and Legal Compliance: every provider file with its rights line read and filed; every person in every layer of every witness, dated; the condition for publication checked by program before each distribution. English. Source in Markdown.
The method of the grammar layer
The method: how the sense of every grammatical word in its place is decided, how it is certified or quarantined — nothing doubtful reaches a card — and what share of the corpus is certified. English. Source in Markdown.
Sanskrit primer
The Devanāgarī script with its transliteration and sounds, how a syllable is written, the ligatures, and how a form is checked. In Spanish and English. Not a grammar. Not certified: no person has yet compared it with its sources. EPUB. Source in Markdown.
Avestan primer
The Avestan script with Hoffmann’s transliteration and its sounds, what Geldner’s edition prints, the correspondences with Sanskrit, and the endings. In Spanish and English. The script is set in the project’s font, traced from Geldner’s type; the provenance record gives its account. Not certified: no person has yet compared it with its sources. EPUB. Source in Markdown.

Copy and change

The whole work is one folder, lecturas/: the editions with their sources, the trainer with its data, the documents, the programs that build and check them. It is had whole from the mirror’s repository, github.com/vocabulario-libre/vocabulario-libre.github.io, as one archive or by a clone. It is changed by hand or by a language model; check_folder.py, in the folder, names what changed. How it is done.

Mirror

This folder is mirrored by copying it whole. To verify it: sha256sum -c MANIFEST.sha256 inside lecturas/. To rebuild it: the programs in the folder, with Python, reportlab and pyphen; the README gives the commands.

Addresses: app.vocabulario-libre.workers.dev and, as mirror, vocabulario-libre.github.io. Both serve the same folder; if one does not answer, the other does.

Install

The site installs as an app. On a phone: the browser's menu or “Share”, then “Add to Home Screen” or “Install”. On a computer: the install sign at the end of the address bar, or “Install…” in the menu. A private window does not install. Step by step.

The first opening needs the network and stores 15 MB. After that the app opens without a network: readings, trainer and files.

Progress is stored on the device. On an iPhone the installed app stores its own, apart from the browser's. To move it: “Save progress” and “Load progress”, at the foot of the trainer.

On Android, Google's browser has the app made on Google's servers.

It is removed like any app. To keep the progress, first: “Save progress”.

An EPUB opens in the device's reader; without one, it downloads.
