Install

The site installs as an app: an icon on the home screen, which opens without a network. Installing is not needed: the site is read and studied the same in the browser. The names of the menus are the system's own; they change little from one version to the next.

iPhone and iPad · Android · Computer

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the site in Safari, the browser with the compass icon. If the site was reached by a link inside another app — a message, a mail, a social network — the page is in that app's own window, which does not install: look there for “Open in Safari” or “Open in browser”, or copy the address and paste it in Safari.
  2. Tap “Share”: the square with an arrow pointing up. On an iPhone it is in the bar at the bottom; on an iPad, at the top. If it is not shown, tap “···” first.
  3. Scroll the list up and tap “Add to Home Screen”. If it is not in the list: at its end, “Edit Actions…”, and add it.
  4. Tap “Add”, at the top right.
  5. The icon is on the home screen. Open it once with a network: it stores 15 MB. After that it opens without one.

The installed app stores its own progress, apart from Safari's. To move it: “Save progress” in Safari and “Load progress” in the app, at the foot of the trainer.

Android

  1. Open the site in Chrome.
  2. Tap “⋮”, at the top right.
  3. Tap “Add to Home screen” — in some versions, “Install app” — and then “Install”.
  4. The icon is on the home screen or in the list of apps. Open it once with a network: it stores 15 MB. After that it opens without one.

Computer

  • Chrome or Edge: click the install sign at the end of the address bar — a screen with an arrow — or, in the menu, “Install Vocabulario Libre…”; then “Install”.
  • Safari on a Mac: the “File” menu, “Add to Dock…”.
  • Firefox on a computer does not install. The site works in it as a page.

If it does not install

  • A private or incognito window does not install: open the site in an ordinary window.
  • Another app's window does not install: open the site in the device's browser.
  • Without installing: keep the page in the browser's bookmarks or favourites. It reads and studies the same; without a network it does not open.

It is removed like any app. To keep the progress, first: “Save progress”.
