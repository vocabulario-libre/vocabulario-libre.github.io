Vocabulario Libre · Main page

How to use

Four parts: what is here, read, study, check. Six terms at the end.

What is here

Fifteen texts by Bolívar, San Martín and O’Higgins, 1812–1843, each in a reading edition: the established text and its apparatus. A vocabulary trainer, Léxico, with the fifteen texts inside it. The documents that let all of it be checked: provenance, policy, method, manifest. The text is public domain; everything else is CC0. The collection grows by corpus; today, one, in Spanish.

Read

The main page lists the fifteen texts in chronological order, with author and date. Each entry opens the text in its edition. The apparatus records, case by case, what the edition changed against the witness. The provenance record (PROVENANCE.md) names each text’s witness and its rights.

Study

Léxico opens in the “Text” view. Click a paragraph: its words form the cards. Shift-click: a stretch of paragraphs. “Whole text”: the entire text. Click a word: its English gloss, written for that passage. The “Index” view orders every form by frequency, in units of a hundred; “Study this unit” forms the deck.

Three card sets: “words” (each form with the sentence it occurs in and its gloss), “atoms” (the grammatical words: particle, ending, feature, order, ∅) and “stretches” (the atom in the span of sentence where it acts). Only what is certified forms cards; the rest says “under review”.

Two modes: “Recognise” (the word is shown; the sense is recalled) and “Produce” (the sentence with a gap; it is supplied). “⇄ side” changes the language on the front. Three orders: “Text order”, “Shuffled”, “To review”. The space bar shows the answer; Again · Hard · Good · Easy sets the next review.

Progress stays in this browser and nowhere else. “Save progress” writes it to a file; “Load progress” reads it; “Start over” erases it. “Save selection” keeps a group of paragraphs; “Link to this selection” produces an address that opens it on any device.

Check

MANIFEST.sha256 lists every file with its digest; sha256sum -c MANIFEST.sha256 verifies the whole distribution. PROVENANCE.md names witnesses and rights. The policy sets the rules; the method, how they were applied. No page carries tracking; only the trainer runs a program, and only on the reader’s device.

Terms

edition
the text established from a witness, with its apparatus
apparatus
the list of what the edition changed against the witness, case by case
witness
the printing or manuscript the text comes from
atom
a grammatical word or a grammatical mark: particle, ending, feature, order, ∅
stretch
the span of sentence in which an atom acts
certified
a locus whose sense was verified; only these form cards
